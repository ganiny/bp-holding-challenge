## Overview

Business Pioneers Holding ("BP Holding", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy
explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, request a quote,
apply for a role, or contact us, and how we use and protect that information. It applies to
visitors and prospective clients in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and worldwide.

## Information we collect

We collect information you submit directly through our forms, including:

- Contact details: name, email, phone number, employer, and job title.
- Project details: scope, location, timeline, budget range, and supporting documents you choose to attach.
- Application details: CV, qualifications, and the job listing you applied for.
- Operational logs: IP address, browser identifiers, and timestamps for the purpose of preventing abuse and rate-limiting form submissions.

We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we do not sell personal information.

## How we use your information

- To respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, and execute contracts you have requested.
- To assess applications for employment or contractor registration.
- To improve the security and reliability of our digital services.
- To meet regulatory, accounting, and statutory obligations in the jurisdictions where we operate.

## Legal basis

We process your information on the basis of (a) the steps you have asked us to take prior to entering a contract, (b) our legitimate interests in operating a bilingual corporate platform and securing it against abuse, and (c) compliance with applicable Saudi laws including the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

## Sharing your information

We share information with carefully selected processors who help us run the business, including hosting providers, transactional email senders, file-storage services, and payment processors. We require each processor to apply appropriate safeguards. We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties for their own marketing.

## Data retention

We retain submission records for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and meet legal obligations, and then delete or anonymize them. CVs and applications are retained for up to 24 months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

## Your rights

Subject to local law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below — we respond within thirty days.

## Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, audit logs, and regular reviews to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe.

## Cookies

Our site uses essential cookies for language preference, theme, and session security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date below shows when we last revised it. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for thirty days.
